I am a colorist based in Munich, grading commercials, brand films, music videos and social media content. For me, color is not a filter applied at the end. It decides how a film feels, and that is where I start: with a look that carries the story.

## What I do as a colorist

I work in DaVinci Resolve and accompany projects from look development to final delivery. That includes the technical foundation: clean color correction, consistent skin tones and an image that works on every display. And it includes the creative part: finding a look together with the director and production that fits the brand and the story.

For [MINI]({{< relref "/projects/mini-aceman" >}}) I graded the reveal video of the fully electric Aceman, matching the paint colors exactly to the brand specifications. For [Deutsche Bahn]({{< relref "/projects/deutsche-bahn" >}}), [Porsche]({{< relref "/projects/porsche-roads" >}}), [Sparda-Bank]({{< relref "/projects/sparda-bank" >}}) and [Dethleffs]({{< relref "/projects/dethleffs-familie" >}}) I finalized campaigns and brand films. Smaller projects like the spot for the [children's hospice Munich]({{< relref "/projects/kinderhospiz" >}}) get the same standard: the grade has to serve the story. You can find an overview of all grading work in the [colorist portfolio](/en/categories/colorist/).

## How we work together

It starts with you delivering your edit. There is an [edit conform guide](/en/edit-conform/) describing the roundtrip from Premiere or Final Cut to DaVinci Resolve. I handle the technical setup and the first pass alone: syncing footage, establishing the base balance, preparing a first version of the look. We then meet for a shared session to align on the look together and, where needed, fine-tune individual shots. After that I either hand the project over for further work or finish it completely, whatever fits your project.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does color grading cost?

It depends on the scope: length of the film, number of versions and formats, effort for look development. After a short conversation about your project you get a transparent estimate with a day rate or a fixed price.

### How does a grading session work?

After you deliver the edit, I handle the technical setup and the first pass alone. In a shared session we then align on the look together and fine-tune individual shots where needed. After that I either hand the project over for further work or finish it completely, whatever the project needs.

### What footage do you work with?

I regularly work with RAW footage from RED, ARRI, Sony and Blackmagic as well as log footage from common cameras. I also bring mixed footage from different sources to one consistent look.
