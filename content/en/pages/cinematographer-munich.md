I am a cinematographer and DoP based in Munich. I shoot commercials, brand films, music videos and social media content, from large campaigns to fast on-location setups. My standard is always the same: images you can feel, and a shoot that runs smoothly for everyone involved.

## What I do as a DoP

As director of photography I am responsible for the image: scene coverage, lighting, camera work and the technical planning from equipment to data handling. I regularly work with ARRI, RED and Sony Cameras, but I always choose the setup based on project and budget, not on spec sheets.

For [BMW]({{< relref "/projects/bmw-island" >}}) I shot social media content in Iceland for more than 30 countries, over 25 assets in one production. With [Audi]({{< relref "/projects/audi-kieler-woche" >}}) I covered Kieler Woche, producing real-time content for social channels. For [Generali]({{< relref "/projects/generali-idole" >}}) and [Sparda-Bank]({{< relref "/projects/sparda-bank" >}}) I was behind the camera for campaigns, and for [Wellhub]({{< relref "/projects/wellhub" >}}) for an international brand film. You can find an overview of all camera work in the [DoP portfolio](/en/categories/cinematographer/).

## Camera and color grading from one source

Besides the camera I also work as a [colorist](/en/color-grading-munich/). That shapes how I shoot: I expose for the grade, think about looks on set and know what headroom the footage needs in post. For you this means short paths, a consistent look from the first shot to final delivery and one contact for both.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does a shooting day with you cost?

It depends on the project, crew and equipment. After a short conversation you get a transparent estimate with a day rate and equipment costs.

### Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes, I own camera setups and add rental gear where the project needs it. Lighting and grip are planned to fit the project.

### Do you also shoot outside Munich?

Yes. I am based in Munich but have shot all over Europe and internationally. Travel costs are calculated transparently into the quote.
